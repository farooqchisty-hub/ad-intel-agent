Colour

Dark-first. Charcoal is the canvas, lime is the action colour, razz is only for the marker highlight, aqua and mint carry borders and secondary accents. Mist is the one light surface, used to break long dark pages.

Degular (display, Bold 700 for headings, Black 900 for big numbers) over Aktiv Grotesk (body and buttons). Both are self-hosted .otf files on the Webflow CDN, CORS open. Headlines are short, sentence case, tight leading.

Signature: the marker highlight

One or two words per headline sit on a razz bar covering the lower half of the x-height: linear-gradient(transparent 48%, #e23067 50%, #e23067 82%, transparent 84%). Highlight the payoff word, never a whole line, at most once per headline.

Buttons and tags

Fully rounded pills with an up-right arrow. Lime filled is the primary action (one per view); white outline is secondary. 16 to 20px Aktiv Grotesk 500, ~16px by 26px padding. The current /lp page still uses square lime buttons; the homepage pill is the newer system and is what the BFCM page uses.

Surfaces

Three card types. Radii: 30 (feature panels), 24 (cards), 16 (tiles and strips), 10 (icon tiles).

Gradient-border panel

1px lime to aqua border, faint lime/aqua wash. Hero promos, closing CTA, "customised solution" block.

Lime-outline card

1px #f0ff42 border, 24 radius. Feature grids and checklists ("SMS Marketing").

Mist card

White on #dbebea, 1px aqua border. Testimonials, feature grids on light sections, calculators.

Illustration and imagery

Two families, never mixed in one block. (1) Thin aqua line-art owls and doodles as decoration at the edges. (2) Flat "-bro" style scenes in lime, charcoal and olive (#3d4a3a blob) for explanatory sections. Product UI screenshots sit in rounded frames with aqua borders and lime callout chips.

Voice on the page

Do
  • Short, punchy, sentence case ("Black Friday won't wait.")
  • One highlighted payoff word
  • Talk to Shopify store owners directly, contractions always
  • Show sources and dates next to any price
Don't
  • Lime text on mist, or razz anywhere but the marker
  • Two primary buttons side by side
  • Mix line-art and flat illustration in one block
  • Em or en dashes in copy
