Voiceover rules applied

  • Professor register: complete sentences, cause then effect, numbers with their conditions, about 140 words a minute.
  • Spoken brand is PushOwl only; the Brevo PushOwl lockup appears on screen only. Code BFCM2026 is on screen only, never spoken.
  • No Shopify, no Klaviyo; the competitor is "per-contact pricing" or "your current email tool".
  • Savings multiple: X1 only, word for word, with its footnote band on screen for the whole beat.

Reusable

HFX motion system

Named components with timing and easing, in motion/system/hfx.js. Every component adds seek-safe tweens to a paused timeline at an explicit time and takes its sizes from the caller, so the same piece recomposes for 16:9 and 9:16 and drops onto the AI live-action films later.

Look. Brevo outlined flat style rebuilt as live SVG/HTML: 4 px charcoal outlines, flat lavender, mint, cream, forest, Brevo green and violet fills, 26 px radius cards with a soft offset shadow, four-point sparkles, mint check badges, violet hand-drawn ink. Tomato Grotesk for display, Inter for body. No pink, yellow or red.

Overlay kit for the AI live-action films

The same components on a footage placeholder: Toast, VoucherStamp, DataCallout, LowerThird, ClaimCTA, FootnoteBand. In 9:16 the hatched area is the platform UI zone; copy stays above it.

  • Each overlay is a sub-composition with a transparent root: place it on a track above the footage, or render it with alpha (--format mov).
  • Always card-backed, never bare text on footage. Enter on the spoken word, hold at least 2.5 s (footnote: the whole claim beat), exit faster than the entrance.
  • The end card is shared by both motion films and doubles as the close for the AI films.
Production

What is rendered, and what is open

  • Built: four HyperFrames projects (bill-16x9, bill-9x16, assembled-16x9, assembled-9x16) with every beat composed and animated, plus the overlay kit. All pass hyperframes check (lint, runtime, layout, contrast).
  • Rendered: 12 style frames, 30 annotated storyboard frames, 4 contact sheets, 2 overlay kit frames, 4 proof clips (6.5 to 7 s each, 6 to 7 s to render locally). Full films are not rendered.
  • Cost: $0. No image or video generation, no paid APIs; everything renders locally.
  • Audio (planned): soft analog pulse bed carved under the VO; printer chatter, stamp thunks, a paper rip on the tear, box thuds, UI clicks, check chimes.

Open questions

  • Voice: which voice reads the professor-register VO (TTS voice or a human read)? Timing assumes about 140 words a minute.
  • Vital Vogue example ads carry their own pink, yellow and red; keep them as labelled examples (as in the approved statics) or swap for code-built mock ads?
  • Film 2 shows the $300 session building welcome, abandoned cart and win-back flows plus Black Friday and Cyber Monday emails; Sales and CX scope is still marked CONFIRM.
  • Cutdowns: do you want 15 s and 30 s versions per film from the same compositions?
  • 9:16 safe zones: footnote band and copy sit above y 1536; confirm against the Reels and TikTok placements you plan to buy.
