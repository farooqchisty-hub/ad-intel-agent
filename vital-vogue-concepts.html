Vital Vogue: first 10 static concepts

A first round of 10 so your feedback shapes the system before the next 40 are written. Written by the agent on 4 Oct 2026 from a sweep of 756 live ads by 17 Indian shapewear and lingerie advertisers. Nothing has been rendered yet: rendering starts only after you approve.

What the sweep found (756 live ads, 17 advertisers, 146 statics and 120 videos torn down)
  • Longest-running copy: instant shape (median 139 days, 11 brands) and proof counts (124 days). "Made for Indian women" runs 194 days but only 5 brands use it: the gap Vital Vogue can own.
  • Longest-running statics: the product or an object under a text overlay (275 to 293 days). A person with an overlay lasts 25 to 44 days, and person shots are half the category. So these concepts lean product and object first.
  • Longest-running videos: tactile close-ups (hands stretching the fabric, a waistband pulled and released, about 500 days) and close-up self-tests (609 days).
  • Short-lived: Buy 1 Get 1 and price cards are swapped every ~18 days. Use the offer as the closer for warm audiences, not the opener.
How to review
  • Each card has an ID (S01 to S10). Reply with what works, what doesn't and why, by ID. The rules behind the next 40 change from your notes.
  • Placeholder figure means a number the brand hasn't confirmed. It gets replaced or dropped before launch.
  • Format borrowed from is the live competitor ad whose structure the slot borrows (never its words).
  • Real product concepts use Vital Vogue's own catalogue photos, never AI-drawn products.
  • Rules check on each card flags unconfirmed claims, body-shaming words, competitor names and repeats. 3 of 10 cards carry a flag: S02, S05, S10. Flagged lines get fixed or cut before rendering.
