10 new statics for "PO - [Sales] - Install - 08/10/25", ad set "Winners v2" (North America + top 5 Europe, $50/day). For your OK before I generate them.

What won (Oct 2025 to Jul 2026, 804 installs, $27 each)

What made them work

  • One money idea per ad. The best images all sell the same promise, "pay only for emails you send, contacts are free", from a different angle.
  • Two bills side by side was the best image ("Which bill would you rather pay?", $17 to $22 per install): a concrete $350 against $25.
  • A real face with a price split was the cheapest install of all (video, $11.70).
  • Speed and ease ("10 minutes", "one-click install") brought the most volume (86 installs from one ad).
  • Huge type, little else. Headline readable at thumbnail size, one button, owl lockup.

Made better

  • Sharper numbers on every ad and one idea each.
  • More native, real-looking scenes (receipts, a system dialog, a phone) instead of flat cards.
  • Brevo PushOwl lockup on every ad.
  • No "Shopify" or competitor names (your rule). The $350 against $25 figures reuse the past winners' numbers.
  • All link straight to the App Store and are generated with the image model, not drawn in code.
