Answers first

Where the videos live

They're on our own storage (Cloudflare R2), not an iframe. The page loads only a poster image per tile (about 20KB). The video streams only when someone presses play, in chunks, so a Radar page never loads 300 videos.

An iframe isn't really available: Meta's Ad Library has no embeddable player and its video links expire in days, which is how the "creative expired" tiles happened. The polished option is Cloudflare Stream: an iframe player with adaptive quality, costing well under $5 a month at our volume. My recommendation: keep R2, and add a proper player (play overlay, lightbox, no preloading). Switch to Stream only if we see buffering.

What "Make creatives" does today

It clones the single strongest live competitor ad on that angle that we haven't adapted yet. That's one ad, which lands in the Library in about 5 minutes and isn't linked back to the card. Too blunt. Phase C replaces it with a creative pack that stays attached to the card.

Triple Whale and Ramp

Together they hold 104 ads and 153 assets in the corpus, 16 of our creatives were cloned from their ads, and 16 teardowns feed the pattern framework. Phase A removes all of it and re-mines the framework without them.

"Make creatives", redone

Card's angle, narrative, funnel stage→A pack: 2 hook-led statics (current framework) + 1 adaptation of the best competitor ad on that angle→Thumbnails appear on the card→Card moves to Needs you→Approve → Draft to Meta (paused)

You can pick the pack size and format on the button. Everything it makes links back to the card, so the outcome of that card includes how those ads perform.

Custom instructions on any card

You type an instruction→The agent turns it into a plan made only of known actions→It shows the plan: "I'll do 1, 2, 3"→You approve→It executes, and any follow-ups become child cards in the right columns

Instructions can only use the same actions and the same guardrails; they never become free-form access. Anything it can't map to a known action goes to "Needs you" with the reason. Each card keeps a thread of what was asked, what was done, and the result.

Phase E · Library and video Export that works, attribution that's visible, video that's obvious.

Multi-select and export

Checkboxes on every tile, plus select-all-in-view. Export builds a ZIP with full-resolution PNGs (the resolution we rendered at) and a PDF copy book: one section per ad, with its thumbnail, headline, primary text, CTA, narrative, angle, source and claim status. There's also a CSV of the same fields. It replaces the current CSV button.

Video concept attribution

New tags on each video concept: Inspired by the competitor brand, or the pattern it came from and how many brands run it. Or Narrative-led with the narrative, angle and claim. Same treatment on the statics.

Play icons everywhere

A large play button on every video poster in Radar, brand pages, Compare strips and storyboard previews. Pressing it opens a lightbox player.

Command Center and Performance both get Meta · Google · Combined tabs. Everything on the page follows the tab: headline numbers, charts, tables and the Kanban's platform filter. Google fills in the moment you finish the treg sign-in. Until then its tab says so and shows nothing, rather than zeros.

Phase G · Economics What it costs to run the agent end to end, updated daily.

What it shows

SectionMetrics
HeadlineSpend today, this month and projected. Cost per approved ad. Cost per storyboard. Cost per sweep. Comparison with an agency retainer ($7.5k to $14k a month for 40 to 90 assets, from the research).
By providerOpenRouter by model (Sonnet 5, Luna) · Replicate image renders · treg by route (ScrapeCreators, keywords, Meta reads at $0) · Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2, Queues, image resizing)
By jobSweep, teardowns, classification, statics, clones and remasters, storyboards, search sets, chat, sync. Recommendations make no model calls.
VolumeModel calls, tokens in and out, images rendered, video generated (none today), storage used
WasteRenders thrown away by QA, re-renders, killed jobs. The waste rate is the lever for cutting cost.

How it's measured

A cost ledger records a row at every external call, with its exact price. OpenRouter reports the cost per call. Replicate is renders times price. treg returns the route price. Cloudflare usage comes from its analytics API once a day. A daily cloud job at 06:00 IST rolls the ledger up and reconciles it against the provider balances (OpenRouter credits, treg balance), then flags any gap. Anything before the ledger existed is estimated from job counts and marked "estimated".
