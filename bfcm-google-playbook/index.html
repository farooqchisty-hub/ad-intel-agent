BFCM 2026 Playbook: Google + Optimization Cycle

One page from two working docs: the Google research and playbook, and the design for how CPMA optimizes the campaign across Meta and Google. Updated 16 Oct 2026 with two calls from Farooq: the twice-a-day rule for Meta and Google calls, and the Google structure now being built. Research only: no ad account was touched to write this.

Summary

  1. Google is 3 campaigns at $150/day: Demand Gen $100 (video and image ad groups), Search $30 (rebuilt keywords plus brand), Remarketing $20, all on Maximize conversions for "BFCM claim" to the end.
  2. Merchants decide and install before about 13 Nov, so Google money peaks 26 Oct to 15 Nov and the last 4 days go to warm audiences and brand search ($6,805 base plan).
  3. Meta and Google are called only at 09:00 and 17:00 IST; every optimization write waits for one of those windows. Exceptions: Go live, the deadline hard stop, the manual kill.
  4. A 15-minute guard watches first-party data only (landing page, leads, Intercom, Calendly, GA4) and pages you; it never calls Meta or Google.
  5. CPMA runs a BFCM rulebook, not its evergreen one: faster verdicts, raises inside an envelope you approve, step-downs instead of self-pauses, stitched cost per install as the cross-channel currency.
  6. Spend stops at 30 Nov 11:59 pm PT on four layers: Meta end time, a Google pause rule at 11:30 pm PT plus end date, the CPMA stop job, and your manual kill. 10 decisions wait on you.

3. The optimization cycle

BFCM does not run on CPMA's general rules. Those are built for an evergreen account (monthly pacing, 2-week fatigue baselines, weekly test batches, 7-day settled windows), and CPMA does not optimize Google at all today: its Google rules are an unused Search-on-installs program, held by google_hold. A 45-day window with a fixed creative set (c1), CPMs rising into November and a hard deadline needs its own rulebook. CPMA keeps its safety layer, the Bayesian verdicts and the $15 / $19 targets, and adds a guard, a hard stop, a Google BFCM loop, cross-channel moves and a conversion feedback loop.

Hard rule (Farooq, 16 Oct). Meta and Google Ads are called only twice a day: the 09:00 and 17:00 IST syncs; the dashboard pulls at 09:15 and 17:15 IST. All optimization writes are batched into those windows. Only Go live, the deadline hard stop and the manual kill may call Meta or Google outside them. The 15-minute guard uses first-party data only and never calls Meta or Google.

What the twice-a-day rule changed in the original design

  1. The guard lost its ad account reads. The Google GAQL read and the Meta ad set status and budget reads are gone; it reads only the LP, leads, Intercom, Calendly and GA4.
  2. Platform tripwires moved into the windows: spend runaway, outside budget edits, disapprovals, Meta install tracking and the Google claim tag. Between windows the platforms' own limits hold (Google up to 2x a daily budget on one day, Meta up to 1.75x).
  3. A guard trip no longer pauses ads. It pages you (P0 plus Slack) with the manual kill one step away; if the trip still holds at the next window, that window runs the stop path.
  4. Approved cards wait for the next window instead of running on click. Go live and the manual kill still run at once.
  5. Stop verification is first-party within minutes and platform-side at the 1 Dec 17:00 IST window. The stop-readiness check moves to the 1 Dec 09:00 IST window (30 Nov 7:30 pm PT).
  6. Feedback feeds respect it too: Meta CAPI redemption events go out in the window batches (Meta accepts events up to 7 days old); Google fetches the offline CSV from us on its own schedule.
  7. A window gate in the safety layer refuses any call to our Meta or Google ad accounts outside 09:00 to 09:59 and 17:00 to 17:59 IST, except the three exceptions (new build item G10).
