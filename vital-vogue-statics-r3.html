Vital Vogue: round 3, faithful clones

The same ten competitor statics as round 2, rebuilt with the Faithful Clone Framework you approved. Only the product, the logo, the words, the source brand's own colours and the person change; everything else is measured from the source and kept. Built 6 Oct 2026. 3 of 10 passed the gate. Rejected ones are shown with the gate's reasons, not hidden.

What changed from round 2
  • The image model now draws only the photograph, from the source with its words, faces and logo masked out, so it has nothing to copy back.
  • Every line of type is set in code: measured font, size, tracking, colour and position from the source, re-drawn and checked against the source's own letters before anything is built.
  • The gate fails closed. It reads the words back with OCR, checks size, placement, overlaps and the face, then two judges from different model families compare it side by side with the source. Either judge can reject; neither can pass an ad alone.
  • Products are Vital Vogue's real catalogue items in colours they are actually sold in; numbers are real prices and verified offers only.
