Audiences

  • Cold: broad (Advantage+ audience) across the 9 countries. Installers (Purchase, 180 days) and current customers (the "PO - Users post Jan'25" list) are always excluded. The creative does the targeting.
  • StoreLeads merchant lists (US 45k to 53k, CA, AU): tested in one Cold · Test ad set as an audience suggestion. They're kept only if they beat broad on CPI.
  • Warm: listing viewers (ViewContent, 30 days) minus installers; install-clickers who didn't install (AddToCart minus Purchase, 14 days); pushowl.com and landing-page visitors; Owl Portal sign-ups who haven't connected a store.
  • Confirmation: the agent imports every audience, translates its rule into plain words and proposes a role. You confirm after the build, in about 15 minutes. Only confirmed audiences can be used. Until then it launches with the exclusions plus broad only.

Keeping the accounts safe

Account lock

Every write is checked against the PushOwl Meta and Google accounts, page and pixel. The treg login also reaches 3 other ad accounts (FDX Sports, Agentsy, a personal one), and all of them are refused in code.

Connection check

Every sync verifies the treg login. If it fails (it can expire about every 60 days), all writes stop and you and Pranavi are alerted. Reconnecting takes a minute.

Write limits

At most 20 writes a day. One budget change per ad set per 72 hours (Meta allows 4 an hour). A 20% maximum step, never past the monthly cap.

Rate awareness

Reads Meta's usage header, backs off at 75%, retries with exponential backoff and never instantly.

Pre-launch policy check

Competitor names, the claims ledger, and Meta's sensitive categories give each ad a risk score. High risk goes to you.

Disapproval stop

Pauses similar drafts, shows Meta's reason, and never resubmits an unchanged ad. That's the pattern most tied to account restrictions.

Learning protection

No edits to an ad set in learning unless a kill rule fires. Edits are batched so learning resets once.

Alarms and kill switch

Spend over 1.5x the daily plan, a CPI spike, a disapproval, or an account status change: the agent stops that lane and alerts. One switch stops all writes.

Meta judges behaviour, not the tool: repeated disapprovals, unchanged resubmissions, budget spikes and payment problems. API changes aren't treated differently from Ads Manager when they're paced like this. Sources are in the previous version of this plan.

Google Ads Checked read-only on 25 Sep 2026.

The active campaigns don't bid on installs

All 6 active campaigns use Maximize conversions with the account's default goals. owl_portal_connect_shopify exists in Google Ads, but it's set to secondary and not counted in Conversions, so bidding ignores it. In the last 30 days it recorded no conversions from these campaigns.

What they're credited with instead

"Install App Button" clicks, YouTube follow-on views, channel subscriptions, and even a MagicFit sign-up (mar26mfsignup). None of these are installs.

The fix (you, in Google Ads)

Set owl_portal_connect_shopify to primary and count it in Conversions, or give each PushOwl campaign a campaign-level goal that uses only it (plus "App Install (GA4)" for App Store traffic). Then remove MagicFit actions from the PushOwl campaigns' goals. It takes about 10 minutes, and the agent will confirm it took.

After that, bidding stays on Maximize conversions until a campaign has 15 or more installs in 30 days, then moves to target CPA at $20. Targets move at most 15% at a time, 2 weeks apart. Since August 2026, a budget-limited target-CPA campaign spends up to its target, so the agent keeps the target at real CPI, never above it.
