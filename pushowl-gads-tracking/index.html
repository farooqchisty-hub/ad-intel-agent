9. Known gaps

  • Unattributed installs: about a third of GA4 installs have no source. Fix: cross-domain linking on the Google tag (needs a tag admin).
  • Portal ignores gbraid/wbraid: iOS clicks without a gclid can't be credited.
  • Matched installs don't steer bidding: Store install (import) is secondary on purpose. Making it primary would change the goal mid-campaign.
  • Ad edits need Google's identity check: the Search ad URLs are overridden at keyword level, so every new keyword needs the App Store final URL.
  • PushOwlAppInstall events carry only the store subdomain, so portal installs are matched through Intercom by email or store.
