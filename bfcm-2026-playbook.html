2. The four narrativesEvery asset carries one narrative as its lead and the full offer as its close.

N1. The Klaviyo tax

You pay per profile, ghosts included. Subscribers who never open still bill you every month. Brevo prices on sends, contacts unlimited.

  • The ghost list: paying for people who left
  • The BFCM hangover: the list you grow in November raises your bill forever
  • The invoice line item: "Ghost contacts: $X"
  • Delete them or pay for them? Neither.

N2. What you'd save

Your number, not ours: contacts in, yearly saving out (no list tax + 50% off annual).

  • Your BFCM ad budget is hiding in your email bill
  • Side-by-side at 10k, 25k, 50k, 100k contacts
  • "Pay for sends, not strangers"
  • Calculator result as the creative

N3. Done for you

A $300 strategist sets up your retention flows and BFCM campaigns, and we move you off Klaviyo. Free.

  • Moved before Black Friday (real deadline: claim by 15 Nov)
  • No migration weekend
  • Your BFCM, handled while you pack orders
  • Agency work without the agency invoice

N4. Ads in one click

Paste your store URL, get BFCM ads ready to launch, plus $200 to run them.

  • Your competitors' BF ads went live last week
  • Product photo in, Black Friday ad out
  • $200 of ad spend on us
  • No designer, no agency, no brief
Proof needed per narrative before copy ships: N1 verified Klaviyo price table (dated, sourced) + an inactive-share figure (Brevo aggregate or a cited benchmark). N2 the calculator engine. N3 the session scope and capacity. N4 real ad samples from real stores. Comparative claims naming Klaviyo need legal sign-off.

5. Foundation assets

  • Offer sheet: one page, the single source of truth (offer, dates, eligibility, code, cap, redemption steps per path).
  • Claims ledger: every number with its source and date (Klaviyo tiers, inactive share, savings examples, session scope). Copy can only use ledger lines.
  • T&Cs + FAQ: eligibility, stacking, duration, refunds, what the session covers, credit expiry.
  • Messaging house: platform line, 4 narratives, 16 angles, proof per angle, top 8 objections ("migration is risky", "I'll lose my flows", "is Brevo as good for Shopify").
  • Calculator engine: inputs contacts + monthly sends; outputs Klaviyo cost, Brevo cost at BFCM price, yearly saving. One JS module reused everywhere.
  • Visual system: extend the deck's Black Friday retail-object system (receipt, price tag, sticker roll) into the four narratives.

6. Landing pagePromo page, one job: understand the offer, click Claim.

  1. Hero: narrative-matched headline, offer line (30% monthly / 50% annual, code BFCM2026), value stack, Claim CTA, deadline.
  2. What you get: 4 cards (migration, $300 session, BFCM ads, $200 credits) with what happens and when.
  3. The Klaviyo tax: ghost-contact explainer, one visual.
  4. Your savings: inline calculator, result feeds the CTA ("Claim my $X").
  5. Done for you: session agenda, migration timeline, "claim by 15 Nov to be live before Black Friday".
  6. Ads in one click: sample ads from real stores (or a live URL preview).
  7. How to claim: 3 steps (Claim, sign up, apply BFCM2026, book your session).
  8. Brevo vs Klaviyo: short comparison table from the ledger.
  9. Proof: reviews, ratings, merchant logos.
  10. FAQ + T&Cs, sticky mobile CTA.

States: pre-launch (early-access email capture), live (2 Nov), ended (offer closed, normal signup). Events: view, calculator used, claim click, by UTM.

  1. Discount duration: 30% for how many months; 50% first year only?
  2. Code string: BFCM2026 (no space)?
  3. Eligibility: new signups only, free-plan upgrades, existing paid switching to annual?
  4. Shopify path: where a Shopify merchant enters the code.
  5. $200 ad credits: what they are, conditions, expiry.
  6. Free BFCM ads: how many per store, which placements.
  7. $300 session: who delivers, weekly capacity, booking tool.
  8. Migration scope (Klaviyo only?) and whether we state a value.
  9. Dates: launch 2 Nov? End 30 Nov or 1 Dec? Any cap?
  10. Legal: naming Klaviyo in ads and on the page.
  11. Budget split (v20's $30k still valid?).
