Vital Vogue: round 5, right sources, our colours

The sources are now chosen by rule, not by hand: proven statics (still running, 30+ days) from Vital Vogue's closest competitors, for products Vital Vogue sells. Every competitor brand colour becomes Vital Vogue's palette; colours that carry the concept stay. Rules in the Faithful Adaptation Framework v3. Built 6 Oct 2026. 6 of 9 passed the gate. Rejected ones are shown with the gate's reasons.

What changed from round 4
  • Source selection: each competitor's catalogue was read from its website and scored for closeness; each proven ad was tied to the one product it sells. Only 9 of 259 proven ads qualified, so this round has 9, not 10. Round 4's nipple-cover ad (R02) is out: Vital Vogue doesn't sell nipple covers.
  • Brand palette: Vital Vogue's palette is read from its own site (plum, nude, magenta, blush, ink). Every colour of the source is classified as the competitor's branding (becomes ours), the concept (kept, with the reason named) or neutral (kept). The gate fails any surface still in the competitor's colours.
  • Our logo is drawn in our colours, never the competitor's logo colour.
