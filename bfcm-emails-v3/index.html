Audience and deliverability

  • Named contacts only. The first name is filled and looks like a person (no digits, not a store or team word), and the address isn't a role inbox: info@, support@, hello@, contact@, sales@, admin@, team@, shop@, store@, orders@, help@, service@, care@, office@, marketing@, billing@, noreply@. LP leads are the exception: they asked for the code, so they get "Hey there".
  • Two tiers inside A, both getting the whole sequence. Engaged means active in the app in the last 60 days, or clicked any PushOwl email since 1 Apr 2026. Inactive is everyone else.
  • Inactive guardrails:
    1. The inactive tier sends 24 h after the engaged tier, and only if that send stayed under 0.1% spam complaints, 0.5% unsubscribes and 2% hard bounces.
    2. Past hard bounces and blacklisted addresses are excluded before the first send. The first send to this list in April bounced 1.95%.
    3. Sunset: an inactive contact who hasn't opened or clicked any of their first 3 BFCM emails gets only the last call after that.
    4. Kill switch: any send that hits 0.1% complaints or 1% unsubscribes pauses the inactive tier until we've reviewed it.
  • Ramp: the account hasn't sent volume since April, so every scheduled email goes to the engaged tier first.
  • Send time: store local time from CONTACT_TIMEZONE. Contacts without it get 10:00 ET.

LP leads: getting claim-box emails into Brevo

Recommendation: a Brevo form on contact@pushowl that adds the email to a new list, "BFCM 2026: LP claim leads". It's single opt-in, because the claim box now tells people we'll email them about their credits and bonuses. The claim box posts to the form in the background before it opens the App Store. That works the same on GitHub Pages and on pushowl.com, so the move needs no change: whoever moves the page keeps the claim-box script. There's no API key in the page, and joining the list triggers L1.

Alternative, if you want UTMs and the narrative on each lead: a small Cloudflare Worker that writes the contact through the Brevo API. Nothing is built until you approve.

The framework (unchanged from v2, tightened)

  • Voice: one store owner talking to another. Contractions always, 1 to 2 sentence paragraphs, the point in the first line. One bit of wit at most, and only if it's true.
  • Two layouts:
    1. - Playbook, for value emails (A1, A2, B2, P3): skimmable cards and tiles, about 200 words.
    2. - Light, for offer, trigger and lead emails: banner, 60 to 120 words, one button.
  • Honest urgency: the only deadline is 30 Nov, 11:59 pm PT, said once per offer email. No "limited spots", no fake countdowns.
  • Subjects: 30 to 45 characters, casual lowercase, a number, date or clear payoff. Three per email for A/B testing.
  • Previews: 40 to 90 characters that continue the subject.
  • CTA: one per email, verb first, first person ("Claim my 50% off"), landing on the screen that finishes the job.
  • Never in an email:
    1. - Shopify or Klaviyo by name.
    2. - "Forever" or "lifetime", credit counts, or team capacity.
    3. - Source lines or footnotes.
    4. - Emoji, em dashes or en dashes.
  • Prices: Plus at $114 a year with the code (normally $180) appears in A3, A8 and B2. Approved 8 Oct.
