Cinematic Ads Harness: a shareable package

A package anyone at Brevo can run with Claude Code or Codex. It takes two inputs, a product URL and a concept, and builds a finished cinematic video ad end to end, with the same system that made Box, Box, Brenda. If you don't bring a concept, it writes five and you pick one.

The two inputs

1. Product URL

Any product or landing page. The harness reads it for the brand kit (colours, fonts, logo), product images, page screenshots for the graphics, and candidate claims.

2. Concept

Either the harness generates 5 cinematic concepts from the URL and the user picks one, or the user gives their own (see Giving a concept). Everything after this comes from the concept.

Why treg for the video, not Replicate

  • Faces: Seedance 2.x on Replicate (and fal) refuses any photoreal face input (error E005, a privacy classifier). We tried downscaling, blurring, stylising and small faces; all failed. A cinematic ad needs consistent people across shots, so that route can't make this kind of video.
  • treg routes to reAPI's doubao-seedance-2.5-face, which accepts our generated cast and keyframes as they are. It also takes up to 30 reference images and 10 voice-reference clips, and makes 30 s continuous generations with native dialogue. That's what kept the voices and faces consistent in Box, Box, Brenda.
  • Reliability: treg's measured success on that route was 100% over 341 calls when we built the ad. Failed or moderated tasks are refunded in full. One token also covers backup providers for the same model (OpenRouter, PiAPI) if one has an outage.
  • Replicate stays in the stack for what it's good at: music, stems, transcription and the speaker check.

How a user gives a concept (shown when the package launches)

  • Let it write them: "Make 5 cinematic ad concepts for <url>", then "Go with 3".
  • Say it in chat: "Cinematic ad for <url>. My concept: <a few lines: the story, who's in it, what the product proves, the tone>".
  • Fill the template: concept.md with logline, story beats, cast, world, the product moment it must prove, must-say lines, tone, runtime (45 to 70 s), and anything to avoid. The harness keeps the user's idea as the spine and fills only the gaps.
  • Every path ends at the same storyboard gate, so nothing is spent on video before approval.

Claims: made to travel

  • At brief time the harness pulls candidate claims from the page (ratings, review counts, prices, free plan, integrations), each with the exact quote. Any claim whose quote isn't on the page is dropped (the static harness's checker, reused).
  • The user confirms, edits or adds claims, each with a source. That becomes claims.json, the only place a number or a product promise in the script can come from.
  • The storyboard validator rejects any figure, soft claim ("instantly", "loved by") or real third-party brand name that isn't in the ledger. It's the same check the CPMA agent runs, without the PushOwl hardcoding.

Motion graphics from the product's own pages

  • Capture: headless Chrome screenshots of the landing page's real elements (hero, product card, pricing, reviews, any app UI shown), cropped to single elements, plus the logo and fonts from the brand kit.
  • Templates (generic, brand-filled): a product card, a claim or stat card, a notification or message toast, an order or confirmation toast, a progress tracker, a feature callout around a real screenshot, the CTA lockup, and a brand outro built from the logo, CTA and store or download card.
  • The concept's storyboard picks which graphics it needs and why (the principles in the skill decide placement, timing and whether a graphic makes a sound).

Honest limits

  • It's agent-in-the-loop: Claude Code or Codex makes the judgement calls (prompts, picking takes, timing, spotting a physics glitch) and the user approves at the gates. It isn't a one-click render.
  • Video models still make mistakes (a spun car, a real-brand banner). The QA catches most of them; some need a short re-generated insert.
  • Music licence: confirm commercial-use terms for ElevenLabs Music via Replicate before paid ads.
