How these differ from the concept doc

  • Professor register: every line is a complete sentence that explains the why (cause, then effect), spoken at about 134 words a minute inside lines, 108 to 114 over each take. Fragments like "Nope." and "Claim it." became full sentences ("...so claim it before then.").
  • Brand and code: "PushOwl" is spoken once per video, in take B only, prompted as Push-OWL with the proven phonetic note. BFCM2026 is on screen only. No platform or competitor names anywhere.
  • Motion graphics layer: every overlay is specified as an HFX component (the system the standalone HyperFrames videos use) with timecode, exact text, animation and position, and shown on mock frames.
  • N4 demo: the picture-in-picture is the real one-click demo with four Vital Vogue example ads, tagged EXAMPLE, so Molly's lines describe what it did for her store without claiming those ads are hers.
  • Format: 9:16, 1080x1920 at 24 fps like the earlier Brevo runs; each page says what a 16:9 version needs.

The Never-Opener

N1 Paying for subscribers who never open · Launch, 2 to 15 Nov

A documentary crew finds Kevin, who has not opened a single email from the dog-treat bakery he subscribed to in two years, and tells him the bakery still pays for him every month. Then Maya, the owner, explains why she moved to PushOwl, which charges for the emails she sends, so Kevin can stay on her list for free.

Ends on: “For Black Friday, it's fifty percent off annual plans and thirty percent off monthly plans, and you can claim it until November thirtieth.” · 111 words (A 54, B 57) · storyboard $4.41 · production about $41

Thanksgiving Math

N2 Save up to 10x (claim X2) · From 2 Nov (approved), peak

At Thanksgiving dinner a jewelry-store owner says she pays three hundred seventy-five dollars a month to email twenty thousand contacts. Her younger brother has the same list and sends just as often, and pays thirty-seven dollars and fifty cents, because PushOwl charges for the emails you send rather than per contact. Their silent dad has the deadline.

Ends on: “For Black Friday, PushOwl is fifty percent off annual plans and thirty percent off monthly plans. The offer ends November thirtieth, so claim it before then.” · 108 words (A 53, B 55) · storyboard $5.05 · production about $49

The Standoff

N3 Done for you · Launch and peak; approved for Meta

Weeks before Black Friday, a knitwear-store owner has shut herself in her warehouse, refusing to switch email tools because her flows might break in her biggest week. A calm migration lead with a megaphone explains that his team moves her list, flows and templates for free and a strategist sets up her campaigns, and she comes out.
