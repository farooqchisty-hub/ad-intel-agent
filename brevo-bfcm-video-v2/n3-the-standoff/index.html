The Standoff

Weeks before Black Friday, a knitwear-store owner has shut herself in her warehouse, refusing to switch email tools because her flows might break in her biggest week. A calm migration lead with a megaphone explains that his team moves her list, flows and templates for free and a strategist sets up her campaigns, and she comes out.

About 63.5 s with the 3.5 s end card (two 30 s single-shot takes; the edit trims the tails to about 59 s of picture) · 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 at 24 fps · 110 words of dialogue, all native in the takes · best flight: Launch and peak; approved for Meta · storyboard spend $5.58 · production estimate about $49

Continuity rules

  • Geography is fixed: the warehouse is on the left of frame with Vanessa's window upstairs; Sam and Jo stand on the sidewalk to the right by the white van, looking up.
  • Same dusk in both takes, B a few minutes darker; string lights on throughout; the van parked in the same spot.
  • Sam and Jo wear identical plain forest-green windbreakers with no text: never uniforms, badges or vests that read as police.
  • One white megaphone (prop sheet), always in Sam's right hand; the laptop stays closed and level; the coffee cup is plain.
  • Ad policy: no weapons, police, sirens, flashing lights or uniforms, and the word hostage appears nowhere (script, supers, ad copy).

Locations, props and fictional brands

  • Hart and Thread warehouse street (dusk, blue hour): a quiet street of small two-storey red-brick workshops at dusk. The hero building: a small red-brick warehouse with one tall upstairs sash window lit warm from inside, a grey steel ground-floor door, the Hart and Thread sign painted above the door, warm string lights along the facade, two orange traffic cones at the curb. Across the street more brick workshops with upstairs windows and blank signs. No police, emergency vehicles or flashing lights anywhere. Light: deep blue dusk sky, warm window glow and string lights, one warm streetlamp.
  • the team's van: a plain white panel van of no recognisable make, no badges, no text, no lights on the roof, clean
  • the megaphone: a plain white handheld megaphone with a grey pistol grip and a round bell, no text, no logos, no stripes
  • Hart and Thread: Vanessa's knitwear brand: a serif wordmark with a small stitched heart, cream on forest green (a painted sign above the warehouse door, a woven garment label, a kraft mailer bag)

Sound

  • native: dusk street ambience, megaphone tone, the door, paper cups, sash windows; all from the takes
  • added: none. Every overlay is silent (decoration).

Music

  • sections: A (0 to 30 s): low tense cello ostinato and soft timpani, mock-serious standoff, very low under the megaphone. B (30 to 60 s): the tension releases into warm strings and light percussion when the door opens, lifts on the street-wide line, carries through the end card

  • ducking: under every line to about 0.4
  • stitch: B's release lands on the door opening at 30.0 s
