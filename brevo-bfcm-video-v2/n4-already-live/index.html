Already Live

At a holiday makers' market a granola maker sees a line at her rival's stall, because his Black Friday ads went live this morning. Her neighbour has her paste her store link, and her Black Friday ads are ready to launch in one click, with two hundred dollars in ad credits; the real demo is shown on screen with the Vital Vogue example ads.

About 63.5 s with the 3.5 s end card (two 30 s single-shot takes; the edit trims the tails to about 59 s of picture) · 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 at 24 fps · 109 words of dialogue, all native in the takes · best flight: Launch and BF to CM · storyboard spend $4.25 · production estimate about $41

Continuity rules

  • Stall geography is fixed: Luis's coffee stall on the left, Molly's Hearth Granola stall in the centre, Trent's Crunch Club stall on the right.
  • Same clothes in both takes; B is later the same evening (darker sky, a few customers at Molly's stall, Trent's line shorter).
  • Phones never show a screen; the product proof is the PiP overlay from the real demo, never a generated screen.
  • The PiP shows the Vital Vogue demo store, tagged EXAMPLE, so Molly's lines describe what the product did for her own store without claiming the PiP ads are hers.
  • No sales, ROAS or results are shown or implied for her ads; "ready to launch" only.

Locations, props and fictional brands

  • the holiday makers' market (dusk): an outdoor holiday makers' market at dusk: rows of wooden stalls with pine garlands and warm string lights. Molly's Hearth Granola stall in the centre with jars and kraft bags of granola; Trent's Crunch Club stall immediately to the right with a long line of shoppers; Luis's small Ortega Coffee stall immediately to the left with a steaming urn. Other stalls behind have blank signs. Light: warm string lights and stall bulbs against a deep blue dusk sky.
  • Hearth Granola: Molly's granola brand: a warm hand-lettered wordmark with a small hearth flame, forest green on cream (a wooden stall header sign, kraft bag labels, mason jar labels)
  • Crunch Club: Trent's rival granola brand: a bold rounded navy wordmark in a badge (a stall header sign, an apron patch, bag labels)
  • Ortega Coffee: Luis's coffee stall: a simple brown and cream coffee-bean wordmark (a stall header sign, a chalk-style menu board shape with no readable words, a kraft cup sleeve)

Sound

  • native: market murmur with no words, coffee urn, paper bags, distant bells; all from the takes
  • added: ONE for approval: a soft click at the PiP button press (about 19.5 s), because the one click is the product moment the audience must register. Everything else silent.
  • split: none: one world; the demo is a picture-in-picture so the native audio stays continuous

Music

  • sections: A (0 to 30 s): plucky festive bells and pizzicato with a nervous edge, rising as the demo builds. B (30 to 60 s): confident warm groove with sleigh-bell shimmer, lifts on the deadline line, carries through the end card
  • bpm: 112
  • ducking: under every line to about 0.45
  • stitch: B's downbeat on the cut to later that evening at 30.0 s
