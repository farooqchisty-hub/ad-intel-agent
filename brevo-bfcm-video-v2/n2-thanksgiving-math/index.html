Thanksgiving Math

At Thanksgiving dinner a jewelry-store owner says she pays three hundred seventy-five dollars a month to email twenty thousand contacts. Her younger brother has the same list and sends just as often, and pays thirty-seven dollars and fifty cents, because PushOwl charges for the emails you send rather than per contact. Their silent dad has the deadline.

About 63.5 s with the 3.5 s end card (two 30 s single-shot takes; the edit trims the tails to about 59 s of picture) · 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 at 24 fps · 108 words of dialogue, all native in the takes · best flight: From 2 Nov (approved), peak · storyboard spend $5.05 · production estimate about $49

Continuity rules

  • Seating is fixed: Nicole on the left side of the table, Danny across on the right side, Rosa at the far head, Frank beside Rosa at the far right corner.
  • Same clothes in both takes (one evening). Take B is later: turkey gone, pumpkin pie and coffee cups, candles burned lower, window darker.
  • Danny's phone slides face down in A and stays face down; nobody ever shows a screen.
  • The only label on the table is Garza Hot Sauce Co.; every other bottle, box or can is blank.
  • Spoken figures match X2 exactly: three hundred seventy-five dollars, twenty thousand contacts, twice a month, thirty-seven dollars and fifty cents, ten times less.

Locations, props and fictional brands

  • the Garza family dining room (evening): a warm family dining room on Thanksgiving evening: a long wooden table with a cream tablecloth, candles, a roast turkey, green beans, mashed potatoes, a glass hot-sauce bottle with the Garza label, cloth napkins; framed family photos (blank), a wooden sideboard, a window at dusk behind; no television. The room is empty: no people, all chairs empty. Light: warm candlelight and a golden pendant lamp over the table.
