The Never-Opener

A documentary crew finds Kevin, who has not opened a single email from the dog-treat bakery he subscribed to in two years, and tells him the bakery still pays for him every month. Then Maya, the owner, explains why she moved to PushOwl, which charges for the emails she sends, so Kevin can stay on her list for free.

About 63.5 s with the 3.5 s end card (two 30 s single-shot takes; the edit trims the tails to about 59 s of picture) · 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 at 24 fps · 111 words of dialogue, all native in the takes · best flight: Launch, 2 to 15 Nov · storyboard spend $4.41 · production estimate about $41

Continuity rules

  • Two worlds, one per take; nobody crosses between them, so wardrobe locks are per take.
  • Kevin holds ONE clear iced coffee (no logo) in his right hand for all of take A.
  • Friends' seating on the sofa, left to right: Tasha, Derek, Lena, Marco. Phones face down in laps; plain white mugs only for the toast.
  • Maya's scarf is forest green, apron denim with the Good Boy Bakery patch; the piping bag is set down before she raises the treat; the treat stays whole (no bite gag: one physical state).
  • Interviewer is never seen and never reflected in glass or mirrors.

Locations, props and fictional brands

  • Kevin's apartment (afternoon): a small, lived-in city apartment: a kitchen counter with two wooden stools in front, a fridge with blank magnets, potted plants, and behind it an open living room with a grey sofa under a blank framed poster. Light: soft window daylight from camera left, a warm practical lamp in the living room.
  • Good Boy Bakery back room (morning): a small bakery back room: brushed steel work tables, trays of bone-shaped dog treats, piping bags, tall cooling racks, a light dusting of flour, the Good Boy Bakery sign painted on a cream wall. Light: soft daylight through a high window, warm overhead pendants.
  • Good Boy Bakery: Maya's dog-treat bakery: a cream and forest-green wordmark with a small bone shape (a painted wall sign in the back room, an embroidered apron patch, a kraft treat box)

Sound

  • native: apartment room tone, ice in the cup, fridge hum; bakery room tone, mixer hum, piping bag, trays on steel; all from the takes
  • added: none. Every overlay is silent (decoration).
  • split: none: two worlds, one per take, joined by a hard interview cut

Music

  • sections: A (0 to 30 s): light pizzicato and soft brushed snare, documentary-comedy bed, low under dialogue. B (30 to 60 s): same motif warms up with felt piano and bass on the Maya cut, lifts gently on the offer line, carries through the end card without stopping
  • bpm: 100
  • ducking: under every line to about 0.45
  • stitch: B's downbeat lands on the hard cut at 30.0 s
