Vital Vogue: round 2 source ads

The competitor ads that are demonstrably working, matched to Vital Vogue's four story lines. Approve the ones to adapt: each approved ad becomes one Vital Vogue static that keeps its format (layout, subject, how it opens) and swaps in Vital Vogue's product, colours, logo, claims and positioning. Their words and brand never carry over. Built 4 Oct 2026.

How these were picked
  • Working = still running and old enough to have survived the kill decision: statics 30+ days, videos 90+ days. Ranked by the agent's score: days live, still live, how many variants the brand runs, placements, revivals and share of the brand's live ads.
  • Relevant = women's shapewear and lift products only. Nightwear, leggings, activewear and men's lines are out, and a creative's duplicate variants count once.
  • Honest gap: proven Indian shapewear statics are scarce. Only 9 static ads clear the bar, and several are catalogue ads. The category's winners are videos, so 17 picks are long-running videos, used by their opening frame, the static composition that stops the scroll. Tap Watch to see the source.
  • Story line = where the ad's argument binds most strongly. At most 3 per brand per story line and 6 per brand overall, so one advertiser can't dominate.
How to review
  • Reply with the IDs to keep (C01 to C26) or the IDs to cut. Each one you keep becomes one adapted Vital Vogue static.
  • Only 26 ads clear the bar, so the rest of round 2 (to 40) is story-line concepts for gaps no competitor covers, such as "made for Indian outfits".
