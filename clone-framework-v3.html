Faithful Adaptation Framework

How the engine turns a competitor's proven static into the same ad for our brand. v3 adds two rules from your round 4 review: clone only ads for products we sell, from our closest competitors; and translate the competitor's brand colours into ours, keeping only colours that carry the ad's concept.

The pipelineEvery stage runs in the cloud from the website URL. Nobody supplies a brand kit, a fact or a colour.

1. Brand kit and Truth Pack

  • Logo from the site header; every product with its price, compare-at price, colours and the photo for each colour.
  • Facts the site states (features, materials, offers, proof, delivery, returns), each kept with the sentence it was quoted from.
  • Brand palette, read from the site's own theme (its colour schemes and stylesheets; the logo's colours when the theme says little): Primary#9B046FAccent#D0A191Accent#B40581Light#F6EDEADark#2E2A39

2. Source selection New

  • Reads every competitor's catalogue, ranks how close each competitor is, ties each proven ad to the one product it sells, and keeps only ads for products we sell. Rule 1 below.

3. Measured spec

  • The source ad measured: text runs and the job of each block (headline, offer, feature tiles, price), type, logos, product, people, icons and its colour palette with where each colour is used.

4. Adaptation plan

  • Our product of the same kind (decided at selection), in a colour we sell, from a photo picked by looking at every store photo.
  • Every block rewritten as one complete thought, each factual line citing the site; text on packs rewritten too.
  • The competitor's whole logo lockup becomes ours; another company's mark is removed; an old struck price appears only if ours is real.

5. Colour plan New

  • Every colour of the source is classified as the competitor's branding, the ad's concept, or neutral. Branding becomes our palette; the rest stays. Rule 2 below.

6. Regeneration and repair

  • The source is edited in one pass on three image models (gpt-image-2, nano-banana-pro, seedream-4) with an exact list of swaps (words, logo, product, colours, person, icons, and any stray text of the competitor's), so the original's design, type and structure survive.
  • If no version passes but the closest failed only on fixable things (a type weight, a colour, a leftover word), it is repaired with a targeted edit and checked again.

7. Gate

  • Fail-closed checks by code, OCR and two independent judges. Only passing ads are kept.

Rule 1: clone only ads for products we sell, from our closest competitorsWhy R02 failed: the competitor sold nipple covers, Vital Vogue doesn't, so no adaptation could be faithful.

Closest competitorsEach competitor's catalogue is read from its own website, and every product is mapped to one of ours only if it is the same kind (same garment or item, worn the same way, same body). Fit = 0.5 x coverage (share of our products they sell) + 0.3 x focus (share of their catalogue that is our kind of product) + 0.2 x price closeness. Ads come from competitors with fit 0.2 or more.
Same product, strictlyEach ad is tied to the one product it sells: its landing page and the ad itself must agree (the image wins), and two different models must read the same product. After the ad is measured, the planner checks again; an ad that turns out to sell something else (round 5 had nightwear read as bras) is refused before any render and dropped from selection, which refills itself. No "closest type" substitutions.
ProvenStill running; statics live 30 days or more. Opening frames of videos live 90 days or more count as a lower tier. Ranked by days live, the ad's own performance score, competitor fit, and a bonus for true statics.
SpreadAt most two ads per competitor and three per product of ours; the best ad for each of our products comes first, then the rest by rank. When that leaves the list short, the caps relax one step for designed statics: spread is for variety, not a reason to come up short.
Designed first, same marketDesigned statics (on-image copy and graphics) rank above plain product photos. A competitor selling in another currency serves another market and counts at half its fit.
DiscoveryCompetitors nobody listed are found by searching the ad library for our kinds of product in our market; only those whose own site makes them close are added, then swept with a pass that asks for designed statics.

Rule 2: their brand colours become ours; concept colours stayWhy round 4 still looked like the competitor: the design was kept, colours included.

Brand colours become oursA source colour is the competitor's branding when it matches their own site palette (a colour distance under 12), or when it sits on brand surfaces (logo, banners, brush strokes, pills, badges, buttons, headline and accent type, icon frames, packaging) and means nothing for the concept. It is mapped by role: the most prominent brand colour becomes our primary, tints our light, deep shades our dark, secondary colours our accents, keeping each light and dark pair so text stays as legible as it was.
Concept colours stayA colour stays when it carries meaning for the ad's concept, category or occasion whoever the brand is: pink for period care, red and gold for a festive sale, green for herbal, blue for cooling, the product's own colour, skin, a photographic set. The engine must name that meaning; a colour that matches the competitor's palette stays only with a named concept reason.
Neutrals stayWhite, black, greys and plain studio backdrops stay, unless the competitor clearly owns one as a signature.
Our logo in our coloursOur logo is drawn in its own colours or one colour of our palette that reads on its background, never in the competitor's logo colour. Packs take our palette in the same colour blocking.

Use it anywhere: the harnessThe same engine, packaged for any agent and any brand.

Static Ad Cloner harness

  • A folder (and zip) any coding agent can run: SKILL.md for Claude, AGENTS.md for Codex and others, the rulebook with the reason for every rule, the traps already found, and worked examples.
  • Runs this exact engine, copied unchanged from the agent; local files and SQLite replace Cloudflare, and every engine improvement reaches the harness through one sync step.
  • Commands, in order: brand (URL in), discover, competitor, ingest (Meta Ad Library or a folder of ads), select, spec, clone, board.
  • Proof on a brand it had never seen: Olipop (US soda) against Poppi. From the URL alone it built the brand kit (USD, 47 products, green palette), read Poppi's headless site through its sitemap, picked Poppi's 54-day Sour Apple static for Olipop's Crisp Apple, and produced "crisp to the core" with Olipop's own facts and can, which passed every check.

The rulebookEvery rule is generic: it applies to any brand added by URL.

  1. The website URL is the only input. The brand kit, palette and every fact come from the site; the site wins any conflict.
  2. Clone only proven ads (static 30+ days, still running) from the closest competitors, for products we sell.
  3. The design, type and structure are the source's. The product, brand, words, colours of the brand and the person are ours.
  4. Our product of the same kind only, in a colour we sell, from the right store photo, in its own cut and silhouette.
  5. Every block is one complete thought; every factual line quotes the site; prices are real; no stock talk.
  6. Our logo replaces the whole competitor lockup; other companies' marks are removed.
  7. The competitor's brand colours become ours by role; concept colours and neutrals stay, with the reason named.
  8. Three model families, then a targeted repair for fixable misses; never a looser check.
  9. Fail closed: an ad ships only when every check passes.
