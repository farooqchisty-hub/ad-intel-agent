The weekly cycleMonday, every brand, in the cloud. Nobody supplies anything but the website URL.

1. Refresh

  • Re-read the brand's site: products, prices, offers, palette, facts with their quotes.
  • Sweep competitors with the statics pass; run competitor discovery monthly; sync ad results.

2. Learn

  • Update both memories (below) and turn them into this week's allocation: ads per stream, angle and format.

3. Create

  • About 70 candidates across the five streams.
  • Each passes its gate: faithfulness and truth checks for anything adapted from a competitor; design and claims checks for originals.

4. Rank and post 50

  • Ranking weighs predicted performance, your likely approval, and variety, within each stream's allocation.

5. You review each tile

  • Approve or reject, pick reason chips, add a note if you want.

6. Launch and optimise

  • Approved ads form the next test batch, paused behind a "Go live" card.
  • Daily: measure, kill losers, promote winners to scale.

Learning: two memories, kept apartWhat you like is not evidence that it sells.

Performance memory (what sells)Launched ads' results score every trait: stream, angle, narrative, format, hook type, source competitor. Next week makes more of what wins and less of what loses; winners become references for stream E.
Taste memory (what you approve)Every approve, reject, chip and note, per tile. Before each run it is distilled into rules (for example "no plain white backgrounds") plus approval rates per trait. Rules constrain generation and filter ranking.
When they disagree (you keep rejecting a type that wins in market), the agent flags it to you instead of quietly following either one.

AllocationHow the 50 split each week.

  • Start at 10 / 10 / 10 / 12 / 8.
  • Each week, shares shift toward streams and traits that win in market and that you approve, within taste rules.
  • At least 20% of the 50 stays on exploration (untested angles, formats, sources), so new ideas keep getting tested.
  • A stream that cannot fill its share (too few eligible competitor ads, for example) hands the rest to the others and says why.

Per brand, set up once

  • Website URL plus auto-discovered competitors give the brand kit, products, facts, palette and cast.
  • Narratives and angles drafted from the site and competitors' angles; approved once.
  • Results learning needs the brand's ad account. PushOwl's history is already loaded.
