Version 3 storyboards: portfolio cut

Brevo ecom YouTube ads, rebuilt as portfolio-grade motion

Each video is one designed 3D world that a virtual camera travels through: depth, parallax, zoom-throughs, match cuts and shader transitions, with kinetic type as a character and glass UI. The real Brevo app screens are kept as HTML replicas. Four AI hero clips per video (Seedance 2.5) sit at the moments graphics can't sell, and each is integrated into the world rather than pasted in. Scripts, voiceover, offer, lockups and fine print are unchanged from the approved versions.

To approve: the four worlds, any frame notes, and the 16 AI clips below.
Clip cost: Seedance 2.5 at 720p is $0.2312 per second, so 16 clips × 5 s = about $18.50 on the OpenRouter key. One take each; I list any flaws for you to judge.
After approval: generate the clips, swap them into the slots, final mix, render 16:9, then native 9:16.

Pipeline test (480p, 4 s, $0.41): the A3 candle keyframe animated by Seedance 2.5. Finals will be 720p.
