Vital Vogue: round 6, every qualifying ad

The sources are now chosen by rule, not by hand: proven statics (still running, 30+ days) from Vital Vogue's closest competitors, for products Vital Vogue sells. Every competitor brand colour becomes Vital Vogue's palette; colours that carry the concept stay. Rules in the Faithful Adaptation Framework v3. Built 6 Oct 2026. 10 of 10 passed the gate. Rejected ones are shown with the gate's reasons.

What changed from round 5
  • Three image models per ad (gpt-image-2, nano-banana-pro, seedream-4): gpt-image-2 refuses lingerie on a body, so those ads still get two tries.
  • A repair pass: when the closest version fails only on fixable things (a type weight, a colour, a leftover word), it is edited, not redrawn. Two of this round's passes came from repairs.
  • The palette is measured on the pixels as well as judged, and brand-colour swaps never touch the product, people or scene.
  • Sources are checked again after measuring: three round 5 sources turned out to sell nightwear or a lingerie set, not our products, and were dropped before any render. Selection refills itself.
  • Discovery searched the ad library for our products in India and added one close competitor (I AM by Dolly Jain); sweeps now ask for designed statics directly.
  • Under the strict rules 10 proven statics qualify today, from 287 ads read. The count grows as competitors' newer statics pass 30 days.
