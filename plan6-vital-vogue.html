Sharper positioning (recommended): "Shapewear made for Indian outfits." Global brands (Skims, Honeylove) are built for western silhouettes and are priced 5 to 10 times higher; mass Indian lingerie brands treat shapewear as a side category. Vital Vogue owns the saree, lehenga and backless-blouse occasions at ₹649 to ₹1,099. Angles to build: the occasion (wedding season, festive, date night), the outfit problem (visible straps, saree tummy, blouse back), the price ("a ₹799 fix for a ₹15,000 outfit"), and proof (40,000+ customers).

Goal: purchases at a profitable ROASThe standard for low-ticket Indian D2C apparel: optimise for Purchase, judge on cost per purchase and ROAS after returns.

BOGO matters: a ₹799 order ships 2 units. Break-even is worked out per placed order, including COD returns (RTO). Each input below is sourced from the site or the market; the brand's own numbers replace them when available.

InputValueWhere it comes from
Order value (one BOGO order)₹799Best-seller price on vitalvogue.in (range ₹649 to ₹1,099), BOGO on every live ad
Product cost, 2 units₹300IndiaMART wholesale listings for comparable tummy tuckers and shaper shorts: about ₹100 to ₹200 a unit
Shipping + payment fees₹95Free shipping is on the brand (policy page), so it is a cost: Indian D2C courier plus COD and gateway fees
Return to origin (undelivered COD)20% of orders, ₹140 lost eachIndian apparel COD norm is 20% to 30%; the site verifies COD addresses before shipping, which pulls it to the low end
Customer returnsAbout 0%Policy is replacement-only for damaged or wrong items, and sale items can't be returned
Contribution per placed order, before ads0.8 × (799 − 300 − 95) − 0.2 × 140 ≈ ₹295
