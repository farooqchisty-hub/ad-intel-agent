Framework appendix

Appendix: Batch 2, copy-led provocation (ads 21 to 40)

What changes from batch 1. Batch 1 won on one big visual idea. Batch 2 wins on copy alone: quiet layouts, one focal element, and headlines a media buyer would screenshot. Every ad sells one offer: clone 3 winning ads, free. Paste a product link, pick an ad your competitors have run for months on Ad Radar, and Brevo Ad Studio rebuilds it around your product, brand and offer. No credit card.

The research behind it. 51 provocative statics: 36 pulled live from the Meta Ad Library on 13 Nov 2026 and 15 from the batch 1 wall. The famous provocateurs (Artisan, Ryanair, MSCHF, Clay, Friend) run no edgy Meta statics; their provocation lives on billboards. The strongest live example is Vanta's rival-talking series ("SH*******T. THEY'RE ALREADY IN ENTERPRISE PILOTS?"), flat colour and no product shot. Backlash hit when ads attacked jobs (Artisan), loneliness (Friend), honesty (Cluely) or used claims that couldn't be proven (Oatly). Ads that attacked a bill, a habit or a category drew none.

The banger test (score 0 to 10 on each; ship only an average of 8 or more)

  1. Stops the thumb in half a second.
  2. One idea.
  3. The offer (clone 3 winning ads, free) is clear in 3 seconds without the caption.
  4. True and provable: every number traces to Ad Radar or a real clone run.
  5. Makes waiting feel costly.
  6. A media buyer would screenshot it and send it to a friend.

44 headlines written, scored, 20 kept. Every kept headline scores 8.2 or more; the bank and the scores are on the Batch 2 page.

Edge guardrails

  • Urgency without lies. The offer has no end date, so urgency comes from loss and rivalry: a rival's ad has run 691 days and counts on, every day of guessing is spend, Black Friday is on the calendar. No countdowns, no "today only", no invented scarcity, and never the word deal.
  • Real, not dummy. Every "before" is a real ad from Ad Radar, with its run length read from the public Ad Radar library on 12 Nov 2026, its Ad Radar page and its Meta ad ID. Every "after" is a real Clone run in Ad Studio on 13 Nov 2026 (about 74 to 113 seconds each), shown as Ad Studio produced it. The two demo brands it clones for, glowday and drift, were redesigned first (wordmarks, packaging, photography, product pages) so the "after" shows a brand that looks real; their pages carry no reviews or ratings, because a demo store has none. Third-party brand names, logos and a reviewer's name are blurred.
  • Clone means rebuild. Every clone line says it is rebuilt for or around your product. Never "steal", never imply copying someone's assets.
  • Borderline, not over. Cheeky, confrontational, a little rude about agencies, guessing and screenshots. Never the reader's identity, never profanity, never a named competitor, never shock without the offer.
  • House rules. No long dashes. Never Shopify or Klaviyo. USD only, no country cues. English only. Type rendered by a real image model; only the logo and the real artifacts are composited.
