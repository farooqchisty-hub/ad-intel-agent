What faithful adaptation meansThe definition the gate enforces.

Keep exactly

  • Structure: the modules, their order and positions
  • Type style per module (class, weight, case, tracking, colour role)
  • Graphic language: banners, pills, badges, arrows, rules, icon tiles
  • Photography: set, light, camera, crop

Translate

  • Each module's message into our strongest true equivalent
  • The product into our closest product, shown the same way (worn, flat, packed)
  • Every brand surface into ours at the same size
  • Brand-signature colours into our palette, by role
  • The person into a new person

Never

  • Print a claim the Truth Pack can't cite
  • Show one product and price another
  • Leave a source brand surface blank or shrunken
  • Split one idea across two modules, or invent a word
  • Keep a source icon under a different meaning

The pipelinev1 stages stay. New stages are marked.

0. Product Truth Pack New

Built once per brand, refreshed weekly. Every fact keeps its source sentence and page link.

Contents
  • Every product (all 11 for Vital Vogue, including the Adhesive Bra): names and short names, price, compare-at price, colours with a photo per colour, sizes.
  • Features and benefits as atomic facts ("side slit for natural movement", "elastic waistband, no drawstring", "microfiber and spandex").
  • Depiction photos by mode: worn full length, worn cropped, flat, hanging, packed, close-up detail.
  • Offers with their scope (which products), shipping, returns and payment from the policy pages, and proof ("40,000+ customers", "#1 selling saree shapewear in India").
  • Conflicts flagged for you (our ledger says 5 to 7 days delivery; the pages say 3 to 5).

1. Intake

Unchanged: the original image at full size, upscaled only when the competitor has pulled the ad, and flagged.

2. Ad Blueprint New

The spec stops being a list of lines. Lines, shapes and icons are grouped into modules, each with a role, its parts, their positions and design tokens.

Module types
  • Headline block, sub-headline, offer lockup, price block (anchor and price), feature row (tile = icon + label), callout (pointer + container + text), badge, proof line, brand surface (wordmark, pack face, label), product depiction, background system.
  • Each module records its message intent: "occasion sale hook", "Buy 1 Get 1 mechanic", "one product feature", "mechanism claim with a pointer to the body".

3. Product Match and depiction contract New

  • Match by product type and use first, then look: nipple covers to Adhesive Bra; tummy-tucker shorts to Tummy Tucker Shaper Shorts; petticoat to Saree Shapewear.
  • Depiction contract: if the source shows the product worn full length, the reference is our own photo of it worn full length, in the chosen colour.
  • Price, offer and picture must agree: the price printed is the price of the product shown.
  • No match, no run. A source with no honest match is reported, not forced.

4. Message Map Replaces the v1 Swap Plan

  • Per module: the source's intent, then our strongest true equivalent from the Truth Pack, with the fact it cites.
  • A feature tile carries one complete feature; a sentence never spans two modules.
  • Our own product names may coincide with the source's words ("TUMMY TUCKER"); the residue rule targets the competitor's brand names and slogans, not our product names.
  • Language check: real words only, spelled right, case kept.
  • Then the v1 width fit, with alternatives, as now.

5. Brand Translation New

  • Wordmark for wordmark at the same height and weight; a mark for a mark. This needs our horizontal wordmark as well as the stacked lockup.
  • Pack face for pack face: our logo, product name and two cited badges, laid out in the source pack's structure and fitted onto the plate's box in code.
  • Brand colour roles mapped as in v1, keeping contrast roles.
  • Prominence parity: our brand's area within 30% of the source brand's.

6. Plate: photography only Changed

  • The image model draws the person, our product (from the depiction-matched photo) and the set, and nothing else. All graphics and type are removed from its reference.
  • Pose is matched as well as the model manages; a different head angle is a warning, not a fail.

7. Design rebuild in code Expanded

  • Banners, brush strokes, pills, cards, badges, bursts, pointers, dashed arrows and rules are traced from the source as shapes and redrawn as vectors in our colours.
  • Icons are matched to our words from an open icon set, in the source's icon style (stroke weight, frame).
  • Pack faces and wordmarks are drawn here too.

8. Type Changed

  • Style class first: weight, width (compressed to extended), contrast and terminals are measured from the source letters and must match, before letter shapes pick the face.
  • The display library grows with compressed, heavy, brush and script faces.
  • v1's measured size, tracking, baseline and colour stay.

9. Adaptation brief, before rendering New

One card per ad: modules, our line for each with its cited fact, the product match and its photo, and the brand translation. Cheap to produce, and you can strike a line before a cent is spent on images.
