Vital Vogue: round 4, faithful adaptations

The same ten competitor statics, rebuilt with the Faithful Adaptation Framework. Everything came from vitalvogue.in alone: the logo, the products and their photos, every line of copy and the fact it rests on. The design, type and structure are the source's; the product, brand, words and person are ours. Built 6 Oct 2026. 9 of 10 passed the gate. Rejected ones are shown with the gate's reasons.

What changed from round 3
  • The whole ad is regenerated in one pass from the source, so banners, badges, icons and type keep the source's design. Only the listed swaps change.
  • Only the website URL goes in. The engine builds the brand kit itself: logo, products, prices, colours with their photos, and every quotable fact. Every claim cites the page it came from (right column of each copy table).
  • Product truth: matched by type, shown in a colour the store sells (or, with no colour option, the colours its own photos show), from a photo the engine picked by looking at every store photo. A struck-through old price appears only when the product has a real compare-at price.
  • Brand truth: our logo replaces the competitor's whole lockup (mark, wordmark, tagline). Another company's mark (a fabric partner) is removed, never rebranded. Text printed on a pack is rewritten from our facts, and the pack's illustration is redrawn.
  • The gate checks product, colour, brand, copy and every graphic device, as well as words, face and layout. Lines the OCR can't read (giant display prices) are read again by both judges. Pose is a warning only.
