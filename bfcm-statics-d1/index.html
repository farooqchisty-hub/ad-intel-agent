Framework

  1. Same approved art, edited not redrawn: the p1b ads were approved with their narrative headlines; only the offer words change, and only the changed blocks are pasted back. Illustration, lockup, palette and headline stay pixel-identical.
  2. The offer is the voucher, readable in under a second at feed size: big "40% off annual" (the bigger number leads), then one small line that carries the rest of the deal and the urgency: "30% off monthly · Code BFCM2026 · Ends 30 Nov".
  3. Proof stays true to the cent: every savings multiple and price is recomputed for 40% annual (monthly x 0.54), rounded down, and ships only with its scenario footnote (docs/savings_multiples_40.json).
  4. No leftovers from the retired offer: no "$0", "first month", "first bill", "on us" anywhere on the image; the two places the approved art said $0 (N3-c receipt total, N4-c scoreboard) are rewritten to true, offer-led words.
  5. Extras stay where the approved narrative put them; the Meta copy carries the condition (they come with a paid plan).
  6. Feed legibility is tested, not assumed: the whole voucher must OCR at 25% size (512 px).
