Black Friday is the biggest list building week of the year. Pop-ups, giveaways, and discounts bring in thousands of new subscribers. But many of those people only wanted one code. On most email tools, every one of those quiet contacts still counts toward your bill. So the list grows, your plan moves into a higher tier, and you pay more every month for people who will never buy.
