Vital Vogue: round 2 statics, adapted from proven competitor ads

Ten static competitor ads picked for proof (days live and the agent's score), format variety and fit with Vital Vogue's products. Each is rebuilt as a Vital Vogue ad that keeps the source's layout, subject, camera and where the words sit, with Vital Vogue's product, palette, logo and its own words. Built 5 Oct 2026. 7 of 10 are usable (R01, R02, R04, R05, R07, R08, R09); the other 3 couldn't be made faithfully yet, and each card says why.

How to read this
  • Left is the competitor's ad, right is ours. Tap either to open it full size.
  • The badge is the agent's own check. It is strict and sometimes wrong (it read our own product name as leftover source text, and a hand as the source's model), so each card's note says what actually needs fixing.
  • Faithful to the source is the agent's side by side score for composition; it needs 65 to pass, and design needs 70.
  • Offers stay real: Buy 1 Get 1 Free, free shipping, cash on delivery. A flagged line is one nobody has confirmed, and it can't launch until it is.
  • Only 4 of these sources ran 30+ days; 5 other proven statics were sexualised or near-nude and Vital Vogue's guardrails rule them out. The newer picks were ranked by investment (variants run, share of the brand's live ads), the stronger signal for ads too young to judge by age.
